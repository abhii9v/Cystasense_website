Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2025

1. Information We Collect

CystaSense collects and stores information you provide to us, including:

  • Personal information (name, email, date of birth)
  • Health data (symptoms, cycle tracking, medical history)
  • Profile preferences and settings
  • Communication data (forum posts, messages)

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide and improve our health management services
  • Track your symptoms and provide personalized insights
  • Connect you with PCOD specialists and support network
  • Send reminders and notifications (with your consent)
  • Facilitate community support through our forum

3. Data Security

We take data security seriously and implement measures to protect your information:

  • Encrypted data transmission (HTTPS)
  • Secure password storage with hashing
  • Regular security updates and monitoring
  • Limited access to personal health data

4. Data Sharing

We DO NOT sell your personal information. Your data is only shared:

  • With your explicit consent (e.g., family supporter connections)
  • With healthcare providers you choose to connect with
  • When required by law or legal processes

5. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data at any time
  • Update or correct your information
  • Delete your account and associated data
  • Export your health data
  • Opt-out of communications

6. Cookies and Tracking

We use essential cookies to maintain your session and provide a secure experience. We do not use third-party tracking cookies.

7. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at:

Email: privacy@cystasense.com

Your Health Data is Private
CystaSense is committed to protecting your health information. We comply with data protection regulations and healthcare privacy standards.
