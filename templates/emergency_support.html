When to Seek Immediate Medical Help

Call emergency services (911) or go to the nearest emergency room if you experience:

  • Severe abdominal pain that doesn't go away
  • Heavy vaginal bleeding (soaking through pads quickly)
  • Sudden severe headache with vision changes
  • Difficulty breathing or chest pain
  • Severe dizziness or fainting
  • Signs of ovarian torsion (sudden, severe pelvic pain with nausea/vomiting)
  • Suicidal thoughts or severe mental health crisis
